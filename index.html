    How it works

    1. Brief → boundary conditions. Claude (Opus 5.5) reads the brief and returns a JSON-schema-validated load case: domain, supports, forces, keep-out and keep-in regions, and every assumption it made. With no key, a transparent rule-based parser does the job instead.
    2. Finite elements. The domain is meshed into thousands of bilinear Q4 plane-stress elements. A banded Cholesky factorization then solves K u = F, and that factor preconditions conjugate gradients on later iterations.
    3. Topology optimization. SIMP penalization, a density filter and Heaviside projection with β-continuation. Optimality-criteria updates minimize compliance under your material budget, with an adjoint sensitivity checked against finite differences in the test suite.
    4. Verify & export. The 0/1 design is re-analyzed in physical units to get mass, stiffness, von Mises stress and the safety factor against yield. Marching squares then traces smooth outlines for STL, SVG and the 3D view.

    Linear-elastic, small-deflection, plane-stress model for preliminary design. Check final parts with full 3D FEA and testing. Material values are typical handbook figures.
